--- Main Notes on Beo ---
What is "Beo"?
An in-depth explination of Beowulf and all it's features / skills / abilities
Beowulf (commonly refered to as "Beo") is the base T10 class of the "Valhallan" classline. This classline is extremely versatile and unique in the fact that it can be played in multiple different ways, with the abilty to wear/utilize any type of gear. Base beowulf also has the unique passive of being fully immune to dragon type damage.
There are 2 main ways to play Beo, the most common (and considered the most optimal) being "Pet Focused", utilising pets such as "Fey Cactus" and "Arisen Spiritgarm" on Beo/BeoA to do damage while you defend and apply buffs to yourself. The second being "Hybrid Focused", utilising hybrid monster from Beo/BeoH (mainly BeoH) with natural hybrid skills such as "Verse" and "Beaststrikes" or using an "All skills and spells cast will use both your Attack and Magic stats" amity, making all your attacks hybrid.
Note: The terms "Pet" and "Follower" are used interchangeably throughout this guide.

Pet Focused
What Buffs Your Pet?
The main way you'll be buffing your pet is actually by buffing yourself! All In-battle buffs will affect your pet:
(T)Att+/-, (T)Mag+/-, (T)Dex+, (T)crit+, bloodshift, all elemental affinity's, and all of Gait's increases/decreses.
As well as stat changes due to status effects. For example "Burning" giving -20% to your attack and magic.

Beo Primary Mechanic: Redlining
Redlining is a major gameplay mechanic that involves keeping yourself as close to 1 HP as possible to maximize the "Valhallan Dissonace" passive. You and your pet will begin gaining --% to all stats begining at half HP.
Ward Absorption is one of if not the most important things for redlining. You'll be mainly relying on the skill Divine Bastion (from Atlas/Athena Vanguard) in the begining to grant 100% ward absorption. That means that your ward essentially becomes your HP bar while your real HP bar can sit down at 1 granting yourself +50%, and your pet 110% all stats.
Accessing and Maintaining Redline:
The main/optimal way to access your redline will be using skills such as "Double Edge" (-10% max hp) and "Quad Edge" (-40% max hp).
        (Note: These can kill you if used at <10% / <40% HP respectivley.)
If you have a uneven number for your Max HP using quad edge and double edge twice will leave you at exactly 1HP
Status effects will be your biggest concern when attmpting to maintain your redline, as although with 100% absorbtion your ward will block all incoming damage, it will not prevent status effect damage as it is applied directly to your HP. The two main ways to avoid status effects will be utilizing gear with "Status Protection" / "Status Immuinty". With status protection only providing a chance to not be affected by a status effect it's highly recommend to opt for status immunity from gear such as "Ymir Vritra Charm" and "Briny Pendent".
(visit "Gear Sheet" for more advanced info on when to use what gear) just not rn as it hasn't been made yet... :)
Avoiding "DoT" Damage:
If you have a status effect or a damage-over-time (DoT) effect like Berserk, and performing an action would cause you to die, one common strategy is to use healing items like the "Small Mana Potion." Using an item prevents damage from status effects, allowing you to maintain buffs like Berserk while staying at 1 HP.
Hybrid Focused
There are 4 different types of "Hybrid"
"Natural Hybrid" | Even though hybrid attacks use both attack and magic, they do not get amplified by both att+ and mag+ buffs in battle. Whether it's buffed by attack or magic depends on if it is considered a "Skill" or "Spell", which can be determined when selecting it.
"All skills/spells are hybrid amity"
"Dynamic Hybrid"
"Hybrid Damage +%". | Skills amplified by "att" buffs | Spells amplified by "mag" buffs

How "Natural Hybrid" skills work
Some skills/spells are "hybrid" by default meaning they will use a combination of your attack/magic and will use your opponents defense and resistance when calculating the skills damage. 
65% of your attack and 65% of your magic, written as the formula, (Attack * 0.65) + (Magic * 0.65)
These skills include: | Beaststrike(s) I -> IV | Lusus Naturae I -> IV | Verse I -> IV
Swansong | --- | ---
"All skills/spells are hybrid" Amity
(Quick note: using any of the 4 types skills/spells above with this amity has no effect)
You can make any and every attack you use "Hybrid" by equipping a "All Skills and Spells cast will use both your Attack and Magic stats" amity. Hybrid Damage is calculated using the formula (Attack + Magic) / 1.65. Or, to put it simply, if your attack and magic stats are equal, making a skill hybrid will increase its damage by ~21%. 
        (Adv. The ~21% dmg increase done is pre in-battle buffs/calculations of M1 and M2)
This amity also causes all your attacks to use an average of your enemies defense and resistance. (kinda)
How "Dynamic Hybrid" skills work
These type of skills utilize either your attack or magic stat depending on which one is higher. The attack will then also change to a skill or spell depending on the higher stat. Having higher Atk stat means it will be buffed by atk+/gunnr, while a higher Mag stat means it will be buffed by mag+/Snotra (this is regardless if it's a skill or spell originally). If atk/mag stats are the exact same, it will simply use it's original stat/buffs whether it's a skill or spell.
These skills include: | Arcana I -> III | Aglostrike | Sands of Aaru
"Eventualus" Apex Skills | Rainsong | *too many to list all*
How "+% Hybrid Damage" works
Technically, "Hybrid Damage" isn’t a distinct type. Instead, it is an increases your attack by --% of your magic and your magic by --% of your attack. There are only four ways to boost "Hybrid Damage" in the game, and these percentages stack additively.
All "Hybrid Damage" Boosts: | Hybrid Monster I -> III | "+ Hybrid Damage" amity | Hands of Selene
Arms of Selene | Certain "Bestial Bonds" | Azure Dioscuri Ring
Beowulf Classline Abilities
Valhallan Dissonance
Valhalla Power +110% | Low HP stats +50% | Follower: Stats +110% / Act +12%
This ability is what allows you to "redline" (explained in detail above). As you approach 1 HP gaining 0 -> 50% all stats "Valhalla Power" is also in effect. This is your pets redline, providing them 0 -> 110% all stats at the same time.
(Note: There is no visual indicator for "Valhalla Power")
Valhallan Protection
Follower Protect +12%
No matter what follower you have equipped thanks to this ability there is always a 12% chance each turn that it will completly protect you from an attack. This stacks with a followers innate protect chance, as well as call of jord.
Valhallan Training
Follower / Summon AI +5%
Follower AI helps your pet choose better skills/spells based on the situation (This is currently quite buggy, will add a better description eventually).
Bestial Bonds
Bestial Bonds (BB) is a (semi-)exclusive ability to the valhallan classline (the sole exception being the ★8 and ★9 "god classes" and amity's that grant BB1) that can do various things from giving you and your follower stat boosts, extra orns/gold, and even bonus rewards from memory hunting. Each follower has 3 seperate levels of bestial bond, how many you have access to depends on.
(Note: the bestial bond bonus only effects the numbers within brackets in a followers codex entry under "bestial bond")
Bestial Bond I:  ★3 -> ★5 | Bestial Bond II:  ★6 -> ★9 | Bestial Bond III:  ★10
For an overall view of the different bestial bonds for each follower check out "Ornapedia's pet sheet"

Hybrid Monster
Based on your level of "Hybrid Monster" your attack stat will increase by a % of your magic stat, and your magic stat will increase by a % of your attack stat. These values depend on the level of hybrid monster you have (Shown in italics below).

Misc Beowulf Information
What are "Call" / "Ballad" Spells?
Call spell's ("Ballad" is just the lower tier of "Call") provide a in-battle permanent buff that will increase/decrease the act rate of your pet in regards to preforming a certain action. action based off which is used/active. The percentage chance of your follower preforming said action is based on the level of call used.
You can only have one call active at a time. Using a different call than one that is currently active will replace it, using the same call skill as what is currently active will cause you to lose it (better described as "turn it off").
These are spells that are only effective when used by valhallan classes, with only a --% chance to to work on non-valhallan classes, --% chance when used by Base Beowulf, 100% chance when used by BeoA, and a --% chance when used by BeoH. (%'s to be added at a later date)

"Tempering" / "Beast Taming"
Tempering | Temper I: -15% | Temper II: -25%
A temporary debuff that lowers your pet's overall "act rate" by (x)%. (Shown above in italics.) Both debuffs stack for a total of -40%.
Beast Taming | Followers deal (x)% less damage
A gear effect that reduces the damage your pet (and summons) deal to the wearer of the gear or adornments. This effect is mainly found from the "Fomorian House of Nocturna" gear and the armor adornment "Manticore Eye." The reduction percentages stack additively but, similar to status protection, cannot reach 100%. However, when combined with high defense or resistance, it can potentially "zero out" pet damage.

--- Follower Act Rates ---
Follower Act Rates (WIP)
Guide on how follower act rates work in Orna
Act Rate
Each follower has base act rate in every of the 5 (+protect) categories
Attack Rate | Chance for a follower to perform a normal attack. This is the only category that cannot be affected directly by gear, classes, specs, calls, buffs and debuffs at all. If not shown - chance is 0%. Note: simple attacks of followers bypass Taming gear damage reduction.
Heal Rate | Affects rate at which follower performs a healing skill. Note, that healing skills like healing wisp (cure + hp restoration with no buffs) will only be used if you have a debuff or have less than 25% of max health.
Buff Rate | Affects the rate at which followers will perform a buff. Ward skills like Ward of Mythril are considered as buffs.
Debuff Rate | Affects the rate to debuff the opponent. Debuffs are skills that do no damage and can ONLY cause negative status effects. Skills that do damage as blightstrike are not in this category.
Spell Rate | Usually most relevant stat, affects usage of offensive skills / spells. (any skill / spell that can cause direct damage to opponent)
Protect Rate | If shown, then the follower adds this amount of chance to completely block an enemy's attack, nullifying both dmg and possible debuffs of it. If not shown - then it is 0%.
Actual act chance
No one knows exactly how act rate work, since there are alot of factors that might influence behaviour of a follower: resistances, amount of enemies, immunities, current buffs and debuffs, Follower AI bonuses, calls/ballads and etc...
BUT: Generally speaking (1 enemy, no resistances / weaknesses / debuffs) total act chance is the sum of all skills and their category act chances.
Example: T10 Fey cactus. Has 3 attack rate and 9 spell rate. Has 2 skills: arrowstorm and arrowstorm 2. Total estimated act chance is 3 + 9*2 = 3 + 18 = 21% per turn.
Act chance from gear, class and etc...
Act chances from different sources add additively to spell, heal, buff and debuff categories. So if we add a freyja's charm (+4% act rate) then total act rate would be: 3 + (9+4)*2 = 3 + 26 = 29%. If total act rate is above 100% -> follower will act every turn.
Note: attack rate number is changed in follower page but is actually not influenced.
Total act rate
Total act rate of a category is calculated by {[Base act + (amity/gear/class act) * Valhalla Power] - temper/jord} * {(de-)buffs}.  Abilities can be part of several categories at the same time. It would be a good question to ask where does follower ai add follower act chance in certain situations, presumably at the very end of calculation.
Follower AI
Follower AI is a stat present on some items and as passive on T6+ (non Hydrus) Valhallan Classes. It adds act chance to abilities in certain situations. Situations include:
When <= 2 ward turns - cast ward turn increasing ability
When you do not have a buff yet - increase act chance of abilities that give that buff
If enemy is weak to certain element - increase act chance of abilities that have that element (excludes weakness from alignments), if enemy is immune to element - do not use ability of that element (weakened effect on aoe abilities)
