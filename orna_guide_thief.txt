Realmshifter raiding
A guide by Cosmo


This guide shows you how to raid with RS, from fresh new T10 to endgame builds with good gear and AL.
Most builds are meant to be used with base RS but should work with Corvus as well, although Corvus is not a significant upgrade to base. The end of the guide also covers Dorado raiding.


I start by describing the general method for RS raiding, then I describe some builds that work for me in some more detail.
General stuff
Redlining
RS’s main asset is High Tenacity, a passive that prevents self-inflicted debuffs (berserk) from killing you. That’s how you can redline: cast Berserk (1, 2 and/or 3) and sit comfortably at 1 HP behind your Divine Bastion. 1 HP is where you’re the most powerful thanks to two other great passives: Resurgence, which makes you much more powerful, and Mystic Feather, which grants you up to 70% evade rate.

Advice:
* Poison and Blight immunity is a must-have, as many bosses will cast Miasma or something similar.
* Immunity to the four element statuses (burn, freeze, paralyzed, rot) is quite convenient.
* Cursed immunity is nice to have, so you can cast Mimic’s Mischief instead of Bear’s Might, but it’s not mandatory.
* The gear that prevents Bleeding is quite specific and not the best to use, so just pick Cure Bleeding in your skills loadout or use consumables.
Specs


* Raider is my all-around favourite for raiding with base RS, because zerk 3 is just very good.
* Oracle is great if you have some very good amities (40% crit damage bonus and/or some elemental bonus). It’s the best spec for flexing big numbers, because you can also add a smelly shroom and still get zerk 3 without being Raider.
* Swash/Blade of Finesse (BoF), glass cannon specs. They respectively offer 50%/65% attack bonus if you sacrifice defence. I use it with Dorado for quick raids.
* Assassin can inflict [t.def down 3] and makes it easier to cast debuffs, including blights.
* Charmer for early T10 RS, before you can make a crit build.
The builds I show below mostly use Raider and BoF, but feel free to experiment with the others.
Pets

 Your first pet option is Chimera or any other zerk pet like Atlas or Great Questing Beast.

Once you switch to Ultimastrikes, you need a dragon pet such as Lotan to cast blights. Bosses who already have an elemental weakness don’t need a blight, so switch back to a zerk pet.

The safest way to start raiding as RS is with a one-handed weapon and an offhand. Then, pick up a celestial weapon (either one-handed or two-handed), fill it with Hands of Prometheus, and make your first crit build. In the late game, when you’re ready to start one-hitting raid bosses, dual wielding is your way to get the big numbers.


No build can be the best in all situations. I routinely switch between three builds, one in each of these categories. And I keep changing things about them.
The fight
With redlining
This is just the general method but the exact order of operations may vary according to your build. The later you are in the game (better gear and AL), the more steps you may skip in this list to go faster.


1. Initiating the redline. Get your zerks and your ward on. Your goal is to reach 1 HP by the time you’re ready to hit. Start with Divine Bastion (eventually after a WoO if the boss is scary) and cast your berserks (raider or shroom). If you’re feeling confident, zerk before DB to reach redline faster.
2. Defensive buffs if you can’t kill the boss very fast and need some safety : Golem, Barrier 2
3. Offensive buffs: Bear (or Mimic), Gunnr, Warcry, Wyrm’s Song (optional)
4. Spam your offensive skill, while checking your ward, mana and status effects at all times.


Redlining requires a longer setup but then you’re very powerful. As you progress, you’ll have the option to speed things up and even skip redlining altogether.
Without redlining
RS Dorado doesn’t have High Tenacity. You can’t redline and have zerks at the same time. On the other hand, Dorado has plenty of very cool advantages: resistance to status effects, starting ward, high evade rate regardless of HP.


So just don’t redline! It means you don’t even need to cast DB, and your accessory slots are free for Naggeneen belts or damage boosting items like Arisen Trevelyan Charms, or Arisen Rings.


Without redlining, the setup is much faster: just 2 or 3 turns for offensive buffs and eventually WoO.

So you’re still early T10, you don’t have a lot of high end gear and can’t make a full crit build? No worries, this guide by Signet and Knight got you covered.

Before we get any further, you should know about another RS passive: Recharge. Landing a critical hit recharges some of your HP and mana.
* This is good because you’ll need a lot less mana pots, if at all.
* This is bad because gaining HP breaks your redline.
There are two ways to prevent gaining HP from Recharge: Bloodshift (temporary buff) and Curse of Sekhmet (a permanent buff, available if you’re wearing a piece of thief armor of Dynasty).

Let’s do some critical hit chance math:
* Realm Strikes has a 20% base crit chance
* Wyrm’s Song gives an extra 20%
* Your status page should therefore show 60% critical hit chance for a total of 100%.
If you intend to skip Wyrm’s Song and save a buff turn, you need 80% chance on your status page.

	US doesn’t look better than Realm Strikes on paper, but it has a big advantage: it hits with all elements at once. It means that 1) all elemental damage bonuses apply to it, and 2) damage doubles if the enemy is weak to any element.
In order to make US competitive, you need to bring    a dragon pet to cast a blight on the opponent and make them weak to Ultima, if they don’t already have a weakness. You also need to increase your chance to apply debuffs, or you might have to wait a long time before you can inflict big blue numbers.


Run your buffing phase, cast Deific Channel and Ultimastrikes: you should one-shot a lot of raids this way (if you’re redlining by the time you’re ready to hit and your dragon managed to make a blight stick).


For a safer build, you can switch the offhand weapon with a shield or katar, or use a two-handed weapon.

Rend / Daggers is not Ultima, which means you don’t really benefit from dual wielding. It’s a great combo with a two-handed celestial weapon because:
* 6 Hands of Prometheus give you a 77% damage boost and a 48% crit chance (54% with the scythe), which is nice because RD only has a 4% base crit chance.
* The best swash armor, Arisen Pumpkinless armor and pants, give a 2H damage bonus.
* Arisen Trevelyan Charms.


Summary
I have three raid builds:
* Dorado BoF, two-handed celestial weapon, Rend / Daggers. Fast setup, kills small raids pretty quickly and reliably.
* Base RS, Raider, dual wielding, Ultimastrikes. Longer setup but then one-shot any raid that isn’t Arisen Morrigan. I use it against medium raids that become annoying if they survive too long.
* Corvus, Oracle, one-handed celestial weapon and Beguiled Katar, Realm Strikes. I use it against Arisen Morrigan because a one-turn attack makes the critical chain increase faster and she keeps dispelling blights.
