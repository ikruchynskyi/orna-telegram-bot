--- Towers Purchases ---
Celestial Classes, Weapons, and Pets
Heretics are powered up significantly with the use of celestial classes and weapons. This page aims to help identify the chase items from Towers so you can further increase your gameplay options. Each item will have a priority, designated by a star count from 1 to 5. The more stars, the more important.
Heretic Celestials
Note: Ascension levels are maintained between all celestials for Heretic. Like base classes, each can excel at some content and struggle in others - it is up to you to use the best celestial for the content you're doing!
There are more than these two classes below! Scroll down!
Heretic Ara | Heretic Corvus
Priority: | ★★★★★ | Priority: | ★★★☆☆
Heretic Ara focuses more on the glass cannon aspect of Heretic, dishing out significant amounts of damage quickly while losing some defenses as a result. It sports higher Magic and Mana stats to help with its' higher cost Ara skills, which are extremely pivotal when it comes to clearing horde content. The newly buffed Ara Vesta III & IV allow you to loose thousands of damage to multiple enemies, and the ability to unleash large swaths of damage on all enemies with the tried and true Sigil Stormflask + Ara Vestaga combo are solid reasons why this should be one of your first tower purchases. | Heretic Corvus creates a take on Heretic full of thievery. Can't die in one hit if you're not being hit! Unlike the other forms of Heretic, this celestial gains the ability to equip Thief gear, which opens avenues to add more Defensive or Crit oriented gear. The increased base attack allows some play with melee weapons and skills, but it is still paltry. Critical Poise opens many doors for increased stats from temporary effects alone, and the magic weapon skills the class provides allow some simple high-pierce options for gamemodes like PvP. Not a super high priority buy, but a worthwhile one.

Heretic Ara generally tends to be the class of choice for playstyles that involve large damage quick, based on its 10% higher Mag stat. For longer form battles, it can struggle due to the decreased survivability that losing Life Siphon creates, but overall it can still hold its own. Played most often with Disciple of Finesse, Oracle, or Sequencer to enable fast or big damage.

Heretic Ara is also the only class capable of doing what many call the "Sigilspam" strategy, where you combo the Sigil Stormflask with Ara Vestaga, attacking all enemies with a large blast before they even get a chance to attack. This combo is great for dealing with summons in longer battles against summoners, but is more often used in Towers to quickly clear troublesome floors. The damage given from this strategy is immense, but needs a full flask charged to use. | Celestial Arrow: A two-turn elementless spell that has a 100% chance to give Starstruck. Charges flasks.
General Gameplay Differences
Heretic Corvus likes to emphasize critical hits often, due to its Critical Poise ability, which often makes temporary status effects that would last only a couple turns last tens of turns without needing replacement. As a result, it can function very well in long content such as anguished raids and endless dungeons, once set up properly. Many of the flasks given to Corvus help assist the critical chaining gameplay - Inflection Flask helps get more uncommon temporary buffs up, Blood-ray helps deal high-piercing damage while still critting, and Manaseep will help maintain low mana.

It loses even more guaranteed survivability than Heretic Ara, losing both Mana Rush and Life Siphon, but could last longer if left in low mana, prime for Mana Feather. This property of Corvus works well with Heretic's innate desire to be at low mana at all times, but is not a one-passive-fixes-all.

Gilgamesh Ursa | Priority: | ★★★☆☆
Horizontal Slash III: A very large upgrade over Sortie, Horizontal Slash III is one of the highest penetration skills in the game and hits extremely hard while also giving a ward turn and potentially reducing an opponent's defense.
Riposte III: A niche useful skill that has a bit higher penetration than sortie and grants 3 ward turns.
Deity Ursa | Priority: | ★★★★☆
[Element]strike III: Not to be confused with the lower tier element strike III skills, these are all very high penetration elemental skills that give 2 ward turns and can crit, but also restore some HP. Useful in some endless builds or in dungeons when not redlining.
Ultimastrikes: Effectively melee's hardest hitting skill, Ultimastrikes deals massive damage of all elements and is multiplied greatly if an enemy has any weaknesses. This is one of the best endgame methods of raiding.
Heretic Ara | Priority: | ★☆☆☆☆
Ara Vesta: A magic skill that can crit and has a chance to hit an additional target, this can be used with decent ascension and hybrid gear to mix things up when clearing dungeons, towers, and monuments.
Celestial Weapons
Celestial Bow | Priority: | ★★★★★
This will be your first Celestial weapon you want to purchase. Likely your second purchase after Dorado. You will be filling it with hands of Prometheus for the Critical % and Critical damage boost. This will be used in a variety of content for you and will be almost exclusively the only weapon you use for raiding aswell as being initially useful to help expedite towers for further purchases.
Celestial Axe | Priority: | ★★★☆☆(*)
This will generally coincide with being the predecessor to your Gilgamesh Hercules purchase. The main purpose youll want out of your axe is to help enable the Chained Shield strategy. Youll want atleast 3 Shoulders of Prometheus with an optional 4th. Otherwise the last 1 or 2 slots should contain your highest quality +ward % or +Att augments. *Note*: For many other classes they will use Selene Hands instead which will take a portion of Mag stat and boost your Att with it, Realmshifters Mag stat is abhorrent so this augment is outperformed until very high AL.
Celestial Greatbow | Priority: | ★★☆☆☆
The Greatbow is a fun addition to the arsenal and works great with Trevelyan Charms and Swash. However, its use cases are more limited and the 1H Bow can do the same work this can, the Greatbow just serves as a minor-medium upgrade in this category. Its best use is combining both the charms and the swashbuckler 1 or 2 passive to give a massive Attack stat that also gets the celestial augment crit boost. Once you have purchased the higher priority targets and if you manage to scrounge up the charms and gear for a good swash build this can be a worthwhile grab.
