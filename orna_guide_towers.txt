--- Wild Towers ---
Tower Schedule | Thursday 0152 UTC | Information
In Orna: GPS, Towers are infrequently found in the wild whereas in Hero of Aethric there is one of each Tower type available at set points in the world.

Wild Towers function on schedule. They begin at 15 floors, and gain floors steadily throughout the week to a max of 50 floors. Shortly after reaching maximum height, they will reset to 15 floors. This process repeats for every tower, but each Tower resets on a different day at midnight (0000 UTC).

Eos: Monday 0000UTC (end of Sunday)
Oceanus: Tuesday 0000UTC (end of Monday)
Themis: Wednesday 0000UTC (end of Tuesday)
Prometheus: Thursday 0000UTC (end of Wednesday)
Selene: Sunday 0000UTC (end of Saturday)

Floor counts increase daily at 0100, 0500, 1000, 1500, 2000 UTC.

In Orna: GPS: 
- Towers are found randomly in the world, and do not replace existing buildings
- Towers cannot be seen through wayvessels
- Tower locations shift weekly at Sunday midnight UTC -- the same time dungeons shuffle their themes
Tip: Use your scouthawk for help locating wild towers!

In Hero of Aethric:
Eos: North Lyonesse, top left of the world map
Oceanus: East Meropis, bottom right of the world map
Themis: South-west Lyonesse, top left of the world map
Prometheus: Depths of Balor, bottom left of the world map
Selene: Dragon's Foot, center of the world map

--- Building Towers ---
Building Towers | Orn Upgrade | Total Orns | Tower Shards | Information
10 | - | - | 15,000 | Through the Titan Workshop, you're able to build your own Towers of Olympia. These Towers don't move, nor do they passively grow like wild Towers do. They are controlled by the player exclusively.

Building your own Towers costs Tower Shards for the initial build which allows you to place a Tower yourself. Towers built this way begin with 10 floors, but may be upgraded to the maximum height of 50 floors by spending further shards and, additionally, orns.

In Orna: GPS, Towers are built anywhere within your Origin Town.
In Hero of Aethric, Towers are built in the custom Titan Workshop area within Asphodel. As such, Towers are T10 exclusive, as that area is inaccessible to T9s.

You can only build a maximum of 1 Tower per theme. That means you may build 5 Towers total: one of each Titan (Eos, Oceanus, Themis, Prometheus, and Selene). You cannot built multiple Towers of the same theme.

As mentioned, upgrading your own Towers costs both shards and orns, but notably they cost significiantly more orns than shards, relatively speaking. It costs more orns per floor upgrade as your Tower grows, but the tower shard cost remains the same at 2,500.

Building a F50 Tower costs, in total 820m orns and 115k tower shards.

Spine | 30 | 30 | 60 | 30 | 30 | 30 | 90 | - | 1 | - | +4% status resistance | Titans drop pieces of themselves when defeated, called augments. These can be thought of as powerful adornments, but they can only be used with Celestial Weapons. Celestial weapons can have a maximum of 5 augments at max level. See the Celestial Weaponry sheet for more information.

Augments can be obtained in qualities from broken to ornate, and scale their stats in the same way regular adornments do. For example, health/mana will scale with quality, but follower stats will not.

Augments drop more commonly from Towers with more floors. You may find wild towers, or you can build your own. Tower height does not increase augment quality, and luck bonus does not affect augment drop rates.

Towers may be found in the wild, and those have a set schedule for height, floor increases and resets. See the Wild Towers sheet for more information.

You may build your own Tower, and increase its floors by spending Tower Shards and Orns. See the Building Towers sheet for more information.

Class | Cost | Total Cost | Class Number | Cost | Total Cost | Celestial Classes can be purchased using Skyshards at the Titan Workshop. These classes are celestial variants of existing Tier 9 and 10 classes, but offer unique playstyles/experiences.

These classes are sidegrades of their base class and, as such, require you to own the base class prior to purchasing its celestial variants. Celestial classes have varying stats, passives, skill slots, and equipment from their base class. 

Celestial classes share ascensions with its base class. Ascension levels and costs are the same between a class' variants (base, both celestials).

After purchasing a celestial class, its skills are free to use on any class and you may freely change between it and other classes, much like any other class purchase.

Once you have unlocked one celestial class each subsequent class of the same tier becomes more expensive, so be careful. T9 classes will not increase T10 class costs, and vice versa. Class refunds are not available.

Mould | 1,250 | 1,250 | 300 | 300 | 1H | 2H | Celestial Weapons are deterministic, craftable and highly customisable weapons. They do not have quality, nor do they drop from enemies like most other items, but are instead crafted. 

These weapons are the sole use for Titan augments which can obtained from defeating Titans atop their Towers. See the Titan Augments sheet for more information.

You can obtain Celestial weapons by first purchasing a mould from the Titan Workshop for Tower Shards, then crafting the desired weapon using Skyshards. Tower Shards are obtained from Towers, and can be refined into Skyshards at a rate of 10:1 -- 10 Tower Shards per Skyshard.

Celestial weapons begin at level 1 after being crafted using Skyshards (not! Tower Shards), and have 1 augment slot. These weapons can be upgraded further all the way to level 20 using Skyshards. They gain an additional augment slot every 5th level (levels 5, 10, 15 and 20) for a maximum of 5 augment slots. 

To craft and fully upgrade a T10 weapon, convert 148,800 Tower Shards to 14,880 Sky Shards.
To craft and fully upgrade a T9 weapon, convert 110,800 Tower Shards to 11,080 Sky Shards.

Celestial weapons cannot be masterforged, demonforged or godforged -- they can only be levelled using Skyshards.

Augments can be slotted in the Titan Workshop, and can be removed for gold, or destroyed for free through a blacksmith's smeltery tab (like other adornments). Stats and passives of each augment can be found in the Titan Augments sheet.
