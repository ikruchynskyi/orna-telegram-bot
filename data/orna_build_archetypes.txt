# orna_build_archetypes.txt - how Orna builds are BUILT: the engines, the stats that feed each one, and the
# recurring archetypes, mined from the 79 player builds in aussiescodex's Build Library (2026-11-06,
# scrapers/aussie_builds.py + knowledge/orna_build_profiles.py) and checked against the guides and the Discord /
# Reddit corpora. Written in STATS and EFFECTS, not item lists, so a new item or pet is placed by what it gives.
# Totals below are medians over the library builds of an archetype (percent stats summed over items,
# adornments, anguish bonuses and amities). Player builds vary in quality; the evidence lists build ids
# (aussiescodex.com/orna-gear-simulator/<id>). Community knowledge, not official.

=== How to build: engine first, then what feeds it ===
1. ENGINE: decide what deals the damage or wins the fight - the player's own skills, Apex skills, the pet
(follower), summons, flasks, ward turned into damage (Spiked Shield), or statuses that stop the enemy. The
class's own effects say which engine it is made for: Follower Stats/Act/Valhalla Power -> pet; Summon Stats ->
summons; Apex Rate/Start -> apex; flasks and Elemental Weakness -> flasks; Collateral/Bastille -> ward and
collateral; Low HP -> Stats -> redline.
2. FEED: stack only the stats that feed that engine (next section) - a stat that does not reach the engine is
wasted, however big.
3. ENABLE: keep the engine running - Apex Saver to recast apex, Follower Act up to acting every turn, flask
charge, ward regeneration for Spiked Shield, low HP for redline builds, status chance for a status engine.
4. PROTECT for the mode: ward, defense/resistance, status protection and immunities, enough to survive the
content (higher anguish needs more).
5. CAPS: per-hit damage cap (raise it with Damage Limit Break), a raid's per-player cap, 100% act/crit, chain
reaching at most 3 enemies - past a cap a stat is wasted.
6. MODE: PvP ignores anguish and crucible bonuses; raids cap damage per player; towers and dungeons reward AoE,
chain and extra turns; farming swaps damage for view distance, horde, avidity and drop bonuses.
7. A NEW ITEM OR PET: read its stats and effects (the codex), name the engine they feed, and compare it in its
slot with the current pick on that engine's stats. Item-granted follower spells (+follower spell) also reach
summons (players, 2026-02-05), so such an item can carry a pet or summon build.

=== Build engine: the player's own skills - what feeds their damage ===
PLAYER SKILLS: Attack or Magic against Defense/Resistance through the damage formula (penetration first),
then crit chance and crit damage, element damage, elemental weakness exploit, faction damage, buffs,
Two-Handed Power (the +10% two-handed amity is a 1.1x attack and magic modifier, so it also raises
penetration - players, 2026-05-06), and Damage Limit Break at the per-hit cap.

=== Build engine: the pet (follower) - what feeds pet damage, and what does not ===
PET (follower) damage - what reaches it (Orna Legends #beowulf-bestla, pinned 2025-03-08): Follower Stats,
Follower Protect, Follower Act, any basic element damage (amity +20%), any secondary element damage (amity +30%:
dark, holy, dragon, arcane), more damage at full HP, faction damage. What does NOT reach it: crit damage, the
"resistant enemy" and "exploiting weakness" amities, The Hybrid, simple-attack damage. Ascension multiplies
follower and summon stats (100 AL = x2, 200 AL = x3, on gear, passive and class base stats - players,
2026-08-12). Elemental damage was made additive within one element to weaken the BeoA Towerfall build
(players, 2026-01).

=== Build engine: summons - what feeds summon damage ===
SUMMONS: Summon Stats, pact power (Summon Pact Power), AL as above, and item-granted follower spells (Sharpen
III gives turn-1 T.Attack III to attack-based summons). Anguish summon stats are large: 5 pieces at 3% at
anguish 20 add ~300% (players, 2026-02-05).

=== Build engine: Apex skills - what feeds them ===
APEX: Apex Power, Apex Rate, Apex Start, Apex Saver (lets Apex be cast back to back; ~80-86% reached with July
2026 gear, up to 50% from Yggdrasil crucibles alone - players/ORN, 2026-07).

=== Build engine: flasks (Heretic) - what feeds flask damage ===
FLASKS (Heretic): manaflask power and charge, Flask Saver; hitting a weakness charges faster and a sigil counts
as a weakness (double charge); weakness exploit applies to the blight and to the sigil (see orna_mechanics
"Build math").

=== Build engine: ward turned into damage (Gilgamesh Spiked Shield) ===
WARD -> DAMAGE (Gilgamesh Spiked Shield): ward, Ward Power, ward recovery per turn; Spiked Greatshield adds a
hidden +20% to shield-skill damage; Sturdy Feet of Prometheus on a celestial weapon regenerate ward every turn
for near-permanent Spiked Shield III uptime (echo Gilgamesh guide).

=== Build engine: statuses - what makes them land ===
STATUSES: Status Effect Chance (rolled once, on the first successful hit), item-granted status spells, the
pet's own status skills (its own chance - see research's act estimate).

=== Build mechanic: chain damage chance - how it works and its limit ===
CHAIN: Chain Damage Chance is an additive chance of a ~50% roll to hit one more enemy; 100% still gives only that
roll, and an already-chaining skill reaches at most 3 enemies (a moderator's tests, 2026-06-02) - so stacking
far past 100% (some library builds total 165-200%) is not confirmed to do more.

=== Archetype: Apex chain (Deity Ursa, towers and dungeons) ===
ENGINE: Apex skills cast again and again. Deity Ursa's meter charges 90% faster but Apex deals 50% less, and
every Apex use gives an extra turn (echo Deity guide) - so the build is about Apex frequency, not Apex size.
STACKS (7 library builds, all PvE): Apex 213 (power + saver + rate), Chain 165, Crit 82, Buff Duration 60.
SLOTS: head, chest and legs carry Apex and Chain stats; their anguish bonuses are Chain Damage Chance (19 of 35
slots) or Apex Saver (11); weapon and off-hand carry crit; accessories carry Apex (Ancient Essences: apex rate
and power). Adornments: crit eyes; Rune of Uthr (horde bonus + chain) for dungeons.
WHY: Apex Saver keeps Apex available, the extra turn after each Apex keeps the chain going, chain spreads each
hit to more enemies in AoE content.
MODE: PvE - towers (Deity Ursa is called the fastest tower class), dungeons, hordes.
A NEW ITEM FITS IF it adds apex_saver_chance / apex_rate / apex_power or chain_damage_chance in an armor slot,
or crit in the weapon slots.
EVIDENCE: xXfBKD (Ymir Feathers Chain), U2ztxx, ce78xh, deQbff, plus 3 more; echo Deity guide; Discord "The Apex
Scaling issue" (2026-07).

=== Archetype: Apex crit (Deity Ara) ===
ENGINE: Apex skills that hit hard. Deity Ara starts with 50% Apex, gains +50%/turn and +20% stats per Apex use
(echo Deity guide); the raid line is "fill the Apex bar and spam 2000 Seals Eventualus".
STACKS (4 builds, 2 PvP / 2 PvE): Crit 162, Apex 186, Chain 45, Buff Duration 30. Amities: 40% crit damage.
SLOTS: weapon and off-hand full of crit adornments (Covyn's Eye, Ferocious Bull's Eye, Steady Hands of
Prometheus on a celestial weapon: crit + crit damage); accessories Apex; anguish bonus Apex Saver.
NOTE: 2000 Seals/Blades hit many times, which gets past the per-hit cap (players, 2026-07-06). Rainsong
(instrument proficiency) appears in the buff-stacking variants.
A NEW ITEM FITS IF it adds crit / crit_damage in a weapon slot or apex_* in an accessory.
EVIDENCE: U6RQd4, Zk2zRs, L3FKke, ZaTfds.

=== Archetype: Chain crit hybrid ("Whipper", any class with a chaining skill) ===
ENGINE: a chaining attack that crits, in AoE content.
STACKS (7 builds across Deity Ursa, Gilgamesh Ursa, Beowulf Hydrus and class-less ones): Chain 174, Crit 100,
Apex 101 where the class has Apex. Anguish bonus Chain Damage Chance on nearly every armor slot (25 slots).
The build's own note: "200% chain damage chance, 95% crit, hybrid ... max 15% chain damage chance anguish
bonuses on all gear pieces".
LIMIT: chain chance past 100% is not confirmed to do more (see CHAIN above) - say so when recommending it.
A NEW ITEM FITS IF it adds chain_damage_chance, collateral_chance or crit.
EVIDENCE: M95W7p (THE WHIPPER 2.0), deQbff, xXfBKD, U2ztxx.

=== Archetype: Two-handed power ===
ENGINE: the player's own two-handed weapon skills.
STACKS (14 builds; Realmshifter Dorado, Deity, Gilgamesh Ursa, Beowulf Hydrus, Heretic Ara; mostly Blade of
Finesse spec; 11 PvE): Two-Handed Power 34 (both accessories carry it in 13 builds), Crit 95, Ward 41.
Anguish bonus Multi-Target Dmg (8) where the content is AoE.
PET: Sharpen III from an item (6 builds) - a turn-1 T.Attack III for the pet; the Beastfelled Cuirass change
gave Realmshifter Dorado about +20% damage (players, 2026-04-14).
WHY: two-handed power multiplies attack and magic, so it raises penetration as well as damage.
A NEW ITEM FITS IF it adds two-handed_power (accessories, legs anguish bonus) or crit on a two-handed weapon.
EVIDENCE: 3twfQN, 7UzyeF, ce78xh and 11 more.

=== Archetype: Pet element (Beowulf Auriga, the pet does the damage) ===
ENGINE: the pet's spells; Beowulf Auriga has Follower Stats +130%, Follower Act +15%, Follower Turn Chance
+30% (the pet can act twice in a turn), Valhalla Power +120%, Low HP -> Stats +50% (class data). The player
survives; the pet kills ("Pet Focused" in the Beowulf guide).
STACKS (3 builds): Element 240 on the pet's elements (Finesse's Enchanted Epee: +20% dark/holy/dragon/arcane,
-50% to the four basic elements; Soul of Achlys: dark + holy), Pet 80 (Eye of Osiris: follower stats/act/summon
stats), Summon 124. Anguish bonus Low HP -> Stats (6 slots): the build starts at 1 HP (redline).
SKILLS: Great Meditation (T.Mag up) given to the pet by the Aaru Robe.
WHY: only follower stats/act/protect, element %, full-HP and faction damage reach pet damage (see above) - crit
and weakness amities are wasted here; match the element % to the pet's own spell elements (Seedhusk's
Yggdrasil's Fury II is dark + holy; a Towerfall pet's element for a Towerfall build).
MODE: raids (Towerfall-style at 1 HP), towers; PvP with Seedhusk is a different, defensive build (orna
mechanics/Discord: stall with Deific Channel, Defend Power, parapet, protect; nerfed in PvP 2026-07-28).
A NEW PET FITS IF its spells are AoE or high-power and its elements match the stacked element %; a NEW ITEM
fits if it adds follower_stats / follower_act / the pet's element damage, or gives the pet a buff spell.
EVIDENCE: nX5Pmc (BeoA + Seedhusk), gVeNMp (Beo Tower fall), HB8V6U; pinned pet-damage list 2025-03-08.

=== Archetype: Weakness flask (Heretic) ===
ENGINE: flask nukes (Omniflask) on a weakness: Heretic has Elemental Weakness +12% and Elemental Immunity
Damage +25% (class data); Heretic Ara raises the damage limit (+400%).
STACKS (4 builds): Weakness 110, Flask 104 (Eye of Ptah: manaflask charge + power on every armor piece), Crit 64,
dark/holy damage 28. Anguish bonus Low Mana -> Stats (6 slots): bluelining (Iconoclast: stats up as mana drops).
Amities: manaflask power, weakness exploit.
WHY: a sigil counts as a weakness, so weakness exploit applies twice and flasks charge twice as fast; the build
reaches the damage cap (Heretic guide), so Damage Limit Break matters.
A NEW ITEM FITS IF it adds manaflask_power / manaflask_charge / elemental weakness, or a sigil.
EVIDENCE: cHADyJ, 88rM5C, BXbZpq, stWc5D; Heretic guide "Omniflask Weakness".

=== Archetype: Summoner (Grand Summoner) ===
ENGINE: summons. Grand Summoner Auriga: Summon Stats +220%, quick summons, Follower/Summon AI +20%; Hydrus:
auto summon and sacrifice effects (Hydrus Pact, capped at 10M per hit and unable to crit - players).
STACKS (10 builds): Summon 166 on every slot (head, chest, legs, weapon and accessories), Ward 50 (Jewel of
Creation in 105 adornment slots), Pet 27 (Eye of Osiris). Anguish bonus Chain Damage Chance (Hydrus Pact has
its own chain code).
PvP NOTE (G.S.A. Summon PVP): keep ward up on yourself (Ward of Ortanite, Divine Bastion) while the summons
fight.
A NEW ITEM FITS IF it adds summon_stats / summon_pact_power / instant or double summon chance, or a follower
spell (it reaches summons too).
EVIDENCE: EqYJrA (GSH hydrus pact), KL7UhE (GSA PvP), LrhKkD, 6TXuGJ and 6 more.

=== Archetype: Ward conversion (Gilgamesh Spiked Shield) ===
ENGINE: Spiked Shield skills turn ward into damage; Gilgamesh Hercules has Bastille +120%, collateral chance
and damage (class data).
STACKS (3 builds): Ward 234 (Eye of Neith: ward + ward power; Jewel of Creation; Sturdy Feet of Prometheus for
ward recovery), Turns 45. Anguish bonuses Ward Power (6) and Frenzy (4).
WHY: more ward is more damage and more survival at once; ward recovery keeps Spiked Shield III up.
A NEW ITEM FITS IF it adds ward / ward_power / ward_recovery, or a shield-skill bonus.
EVIDENCE: RzUDjD (Hercules Build Spiked Shield, the library's most voted, 7), XcQAn3, uz4fLE; echo Gilgamesh
guide.

=== Archetype: Status support (raids: stasis or petrify + sigil) ===
ENGINE: stop the boss (stasis acts like petrify - the boss cannot act) while a damage dealer hits; the pet adds
a sigil (Dark Sigil). Chronomancer's Lacuna II has a 50% stasis chance; Naggeneen's Belt makes stasis land
easier and last 3+ turns (Reddit, u/Widogeist).
STACKS (3 builds): Status Effect Chance 166 (Poison Quill: status chance + status protection), Status
Protection 88, Pet 58 (Severause's Cabochon: follower act + pact power).
MODE: duo raids (a stasis user plus a damage dealer); a few raids are immune to stasis.
A NEW ITEM FITS IF it adds status_effect_chance, a stasis/petrify skill, or a sigil for the pet.
EVIDENCE: N8ftxT, pYg3a6, 9mDYmd; Discord "Stasis abuse" (2026-01).

=== Archetype: Redline crit (Realmshifter Corvus) ===
ENGINE: the player's crits at low HP. Realmshifter: Low HP -> Stats +100% (Resurgence), damage to ward +125%,
dodge up to 60% at low HP (class data); Corvus adds Critical Chain and crit-status fade.
STACKS (5 builds): Crit 136 (Eye of Sekhmet, Ferocious Bull's Eye, Steady Hands of Prometheus), Faction 25,
Low HP 20, Bypass 15. Pet skill Riposte in 2.
WHY: redlining doubles stats; Crit Poise keeps crit buffs up across long raid fights (Realmshifter thread
2026-04).
A NEW ITEM FITS IF it adds crit / crit_damage / low_hp_stats / ward bypass.
EVIDENCE: GLfHwT, xPTFAJ, MxGngW, 6DapAt.

=== Archetype: Farming loadout ===
ENGINE: not damage - finding and finishing more content.
STACKS (12 builds, any class): Farm 200 (view distance - Symbol of War adornments; dungeon horde bonus - Rune of
Uthr; avidity; drop and material bonuses), with enough Chain and Crit to clear hordes. Each view-distance
source adds a flat 20% (Reddit, u/Widogeist).
NOTE: keep separate loadouts - the world loadout for view distance, raid/PvP loadouts for damage and survival.
EVIDENCE: R9Ba5Z (Horde boss farming, 5 votes), MbeTsN, j7qyFq and 9 more.

=== Archetype: Defensive PvP ===
ENGINE: survive and win the long fight (counters, collateral, ward, a pet that stalls).
STACKS (7 PvP builds: Gilgamesh, Deity Ursa, Grand Summoner Auriga): Ward 107 (Eye of Neith, Immortal's Jewel),
little crit/apex/chain. NOTE: these builds still carry anguish bonuses (Ward Power, Frenzy), but anguish and
crucible bonuses do not work in PvP (players, 2026-08) - do not count them for a PvP pick.
A NEW ITEM FITS IF it adds ward, defense/resistance, status protection or a counter/collateral effect that
works in PvP.
EVIDENCE: RzUDjD, LbHypb, TmnB8P, bnVxMD and 3 more.
