FAQ
Q: | Where do i get Buggane or other event summons?
A: | Buggane can be bought at the Arcanist and Monuments Guild Shop during a Paths of Fomoria Event. Check the Skills tab for infos about every event summon. Also all Fomorian and Halloween event summons can be bought at the Arcanist and Monuments Guild Shop during The Mimics Are Loose, Return of the Mimics and the Friends and Family december event.
Q: | Is my build good? What can i improve?
A: | As long as you can make progress your build is just fine. If you're looking for specific gear or an orientation on what to build check the Classine Gear, Noteworthy Gear and Builds tab.
Q: | I feel weak... should i upgrade my gear even when my summons do the work?
A: | Yes absolutely. Upgrade your gear during your sleep to not miss out on it during the day. It really works wonders for your survivability. A good upgraded weapon even enables you to do dmg by yourself! The upgrades up until Lvl 10 are relatively cheap so don't worry about the costs.
Q: | My minions shall become stronger! How do summon stats work?
A: | Summon stats are additive except for Ascensions. They influence the stats of your summons multiplicative. The general formula is:
(100% + Summoner's passive + Specialization + Gear)*(1+Ascensions/100)
An example with GS Benefactor at Ascension Level 10 and summon stat armor:
(100% + 200% + 130% + 37%)* (1+10/100)= 467%* 1.10= 513.7%
Q: | Scrap the boring math! Does summon stat gear matter or not?
A: | Due to their additive nature the impact of summon stat gear decreases the stronger your summoner passives become. Even moreso summoner gear does tend to have bad stats impairing your survivability thus the general recommendation to neglect summon stat gear and to build 'tanky' instead. Check the Abilities and Gear tabs for values.
To further explain in the example above the summon stat gear increases the overall stat by 37/430=8.6% instead of 37% so roughly a quarter of the shown value.
Q: | I need summons! Now! How do Benefactor, Sturdy Feet of Eos and Omega Riftlock work together?
A: | Benefactor has a 50% chance to reduce your summon turns by 1 turn.
Each Sturdy Feet of Eos works like Benefactor giving you a chance of 50% to reduce your summon turns by 1 turn and they stack. So 2 augments give you a chance of 50% to reduce your summon turns by 2 turns etc.
Each Omega Riftlock gives you a chance of 25% to instantly summon and they stack so two Omega Riftlocks give you a 50% chance.
All of those stack. For example Benefactor and 2 Sturdy Feet of Eos result in a 50% chance to reduce your summon turns by 3 turns possibly instantly summoning a 4 turn summon. This does stack with up to two Omega Riftlocks too resulting in a 75% chance to instantly summon a 4 turn summon!
Q: | Let's talk towers. Do i want to build a tower? Which one?
A: | If you have ongoing trouble to find towers in the wild it's beneficial to build one on your own to be independent of the weekly rotation. With the new home teleport feature you're able to climb your own tower wherever and whenever you want!
For the summoner classline the Tower of Eos does provide you with the basic Titan augments you need. When diving into advanced tactics the Towers of Prometheus, Oceanus, Selene and Themis in this order become interesting too.
Q: | The question of questions: Celestial Weapons or Celestial Classes first?
A: | Buy the classes first except Summoner Hydrus. Summoner Auriga's Mighty Griffin is a very good and consistent multi purpose summon, Grand Summoner Hydrus becomes less relevant on its own with ascensions moving forward but its Ancient Dragon summon is arguably the best summon in the game and Grand Summoner Auriga provides you with warrior gear and smart summons making your plays much safer. Summoner Hydrus has nothing to offer unfortunately since its summon ain't that useful.
After unlocking the classes you should have a good understanding on what weapon you need to supplement your favourite playstyle and enough augments to build it as you like.
Q: | Can i codex as a summoner?
A: | First things first your summons can't codex! Meaning resistances, weaknesses, immunities etc. your summons discover during a fight will not be implemented in your codex.
In theory you can codex as a summoner by using various skills and spells on your own but you'll have to put much more effort behind it than classes that are able to use a follower. Also some status immunities can only be codexed with a certain follower. So in order to successfully codex a class that's able to use a follower is much more effective.
Q: | Unsolved Mystery. How does Battalions exactly work?
A: | In general Battlions gives your summons a chance to protect you from incoming spells or skills and take the damage and effects themselves. The higher the ability level and the number of summons on the field the higher are the chances of you being protected. The chances can further be increased by certain gear or adornments, check the Classline and Noteworthy Gear tabs.
After the latest 'hidden numbers' update we do have some exact numbers concerning the chances. However some informations are still unknown.
1.) Battalions 3's chances do cap at 50% in PvE content. Note that the enemy AI has more targets to choose from the more summons are on the field further decreasing the chances of you being hit.
2.) The individual chances to be protected per summon are influenced by diminishing returns meaning every consecutive summon will have less chance to protect you than the previous one. Check the values of each passive at the Skills tab.
3.) It's possible to double the summon protect chances of Battalions with gear.
4.) Summon protect chances cannot reach 100%.
5.) Summon protect chances are greatly reduced in PvP/PvAI.
Q: | What's going on with Summoner/ Grand Summoner Hydrus?
A: | The pact oriented subclasses of the classline had a massive rework in late 2025 bringing back the power to the player.
They passively summon hordes of weak summons to sacrifice them with their new Hydrus Pact and harvest huge benefits from it. Also due to their increased  stats and temporary buffs they're comfortably able to take a hit opposed to their peers.
Charon Ritual has a chance to automatically summon summons from your spell loadout. It's able to summon multiple entities at once if the spell would do so. Also it may prevent you from falling in battle at the cost of your summons.
Hydrus Pact sacrifices up to 3 summons to deal massive damage to up to 3 targets. While doing so it gives ward turns.
The Hydrus passive grants you a stat boost per fallen summon maxing at 10 fallen summons. Additionally you have a chance to get many temporary buffs when sacrificing summons.

How to play? | Summon Dead to fill the field. Use Sacrificial Pact 2 to wipe out your summons and stack the Hydrus passive. Repeat until it's at 100%. It will stay charged on Hydrus even if switching classes during your setup. | Cast Divine Bastion 2 first and use Ward of Ortanite to create about 300 ward turns with the help of your follower, more if you want to go deeper. | Summon 5 Ancient Dragons or Bone Mammoth with max summon stats. | Activate Ophion Stance and spread Wyvern Speed 2, Jinn's Talent, Barrier 2 and Golem's Fortitude to your summons. Those are not guaranteed to go over and that's okay. Now use Gait of Snotra to spread those too. Deactivate Ophion Stance and give yourself Gait of Thrud to override Snotra. Activate Ophion again and cast Gait of Snotra for another chance of spreading Snotra to your summons. Repeat until all summons and yourself have Gait of Snotra. Turn Ophion Stance off and make sure you yourself have Snotra too. You might want to start and end with Thrud if you use Mammoth to slighty buff att and mag instead. | Spread Asteria Stance by toggling it on and off again. Every toggle has a chance of spreading it to a random summon and either turning it on or off on them. Due to a whole lot of randomness involved you want to make sure atleast 3 summons have the stance. If you feel very lucky you might go for more. | Use Sonata of Magic to give your summons mag^ and mag^^ (and Sonata of Power when using Mammoth). The latter has a chance of 20% to procc so it might take some time. If you feel like it you might use Sonata of Resistance on them too for res^ and res^^. | Give Prism Wall to yourself and all of your summons. After doing so activate Ophion Stance. | If you want to use Berserk on your summons use Berserk for the following step, if not use Ward of Ortanite. Make sure Ophion Stance is active. Use a spell until you get the Dragon Alignment (Earth for Mammoth). Your Fomorian gear has a chance to spread Berserk if you're using it as a spell. Once you're aligned switch to your Book of Fire X. Use a spell until you got the Fire Alignment. Switch back to your Dragon (Earth for Mammoth) Book X and repeat until all of your summons are aligned by one or the other. If you wish keep using Berserk until all of your summons have it. Repeat as Raider with Berserk 3 if you want even more dmg (and self inflicted dmg). | If you want to be as tanky as possible activate Aegir Stance and cast Gait of Hildr. Note that your ability to use Life Pact on your summons and thus heal them will heavily suffer under this step. | From here on you have many options. GSA has smart AI but a bad Life Pact 2, GSH is the tankiest and has the best Life Pact 2 but only Battalions 2, GS is in between the two in terms of pacts and Battalions. Note that the Bone Mammoth needs AI thus GSA to work properly. Oracle gives the most profit but Assassin makes sure you hit your debuffs. On your run use Stun Dart on all Bosses that can be stunned, use Provoke, Ponder and Rhada Pact 2 them to your summons. Debuff strong opponents, heal your summons, if one dies change to Inquisitor and revive it on the same floor or it's lost. Keep your ward up and your head down. Good luck! | Life Pact 2 scales with your HP, your pact multiplier and your mag buffs. To reach the maximum amount of heal we need to stack as much HP and pact multipliers as possible, given your mag buffs are intact. If your summons are low, switch to Grand Summoner Hydrus and drop a massive Life Pact 2. Make sure to not unalive yourself.

Why is it important? | Yelmogus gear in combination with Ophion Stance allows you to reliably transfer permanent buffs to your summons (estimate 95% chance, 100% when switching to Yel Staff after turning on Ophion Stance). That means that buffing Snotra and the steps 3 to 5 of the Arisen Great Gazer guide and steps 3 to 7 (except when Wintara gear is required at step 4 and 7) of the Ancient Dragon/ Bone Mammoth guide become significantly easier.
Yelmogus gear is also best in slot in terms of summon stats so use it if you have it to set up your summons.
